---
title: "Make the next action clear"
description: "A useful operating note helps a small team decide what to do next."
pubDate: 2026-10-04
---
A small team does not need another dashboard to discover that work is waiting. It needs a clear next action, an owner, and evidence that the action helped.

## Keep evidence close to the decision

Write down the observation, the decision it supports, and what would make you change your mind. Revisit that note after the work ships.
